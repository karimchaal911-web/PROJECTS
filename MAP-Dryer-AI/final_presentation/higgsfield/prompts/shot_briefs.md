# CINEMATIC PLATE BRIEFS — WRITTEN, NOT EXECUTED

Three shots were specified before the decision recorded in `../README.md` to use
real photography and procedural geometry instead. They are kept so the choice is
auditable and so the briefs are ready if the decision is ever revisited.

Each follows the required workflow: real reference → engineering constraint →
narrative purpose → camera → lens → lighting → movement → forbidden changes.

---

## PLATE 01 — INDUSTRIAL ESTABLISHING (scene 01 alternative)

* **Reference:** `assets/process/drying_section_structure.jpeg`,
  `assets/original/jorf_lasfar_industrial_platform.png`
* **Engineering constraint:** A phosphate-processing plant, not a refinery and
  not a power station. Green-painted structural steel, insulated vertical
  columns, grating walkways with yellow handrails, dust-loaded surfaces. No
  flare stacks, no cooling towers, no visible flame.
* **Narrative purpose:** Establish scale and the fact that the process is
  already running before a single word is spoken.
* **Camera:** Slow lateral drift, left to right, at roughly 40 m altitude.
* **Lens:** 35 mm equivalent. No fisheye, no vertical distortion.
* **Lighting:** Pre-dawn. Low key from camera-left, cool ambient, warm process
  lighting from within the structure. No sun in frame.
* **Movement:** 6 s, constant velocity, no acceleration, no parallax stunts.
* **Forbidden:** Personnel, vehicles, branding, signage, flame, steam plumes
  large enough to read as emissions, any equipment not present in the reference.

**Rejected because** scene 01 must be the same live 3D world as scene 14 for the
closing rhyme to land.

## PLATE 02 — HERO DRYER (scene 04 alternative)

* **Reference:** `assets/process/rotary_dryer_shell.jpeg`
* **Engineering constraint:** A horizontal, slightly inclined rotary drum on two
  riding rings carried by trunnion rollers, with a girth-gear drive, a feed
  chute at the high end and a discharge hood at the low end. Pale grey steel
  under MAP dust. **No kiln flame, no visible burner, no refractory lining.**
* **Narrative purpose:** Make the machine feel physically large and genuinely in
  motion before the argument becomes analytical.
* **Camera:** Slow arc, 20° of travel, holding the drum axis in frame.
* **Lens:** 50 mm equivalent for honest proportion.
* **Lighting:** Single hard key from above camera-left, cool rim from behind
  right, warm fill spilling from the discharge hood.
* **Movement:** 8 s, ease-in-ease-out, settling on a three-quarter view.
* **Forbidden:** Any alteration to drum proportion, ring count or support
  geometry; any glow implying combustion inside the shell.

**Rejected because** accuracy of this specific machine is the presentation's
central claim, and because the drum must keep turning continuously across
fourteen scenes — which a fixed clip cannot do.

## PLATE 03 — MATERIAL MACRO (scene 02 alternative)

* **Reference:** `assets/process/soluble_map_storage.jpeg`
* **Engineering constraint:** Off-white to pale-cream crystalline granules,
  irregular faceting, 2–4 mm, matte with slight translucency. **Not spherical
  prills, not white powder, not coloured.**
* **Narrative purpose:** Give the product a physical identity before it becomes
  a data point.
* **Camera:** Static, shallow depth of field, granule occupying the right third.
* **Lens:** 100 mm macro equivalent.
* **Lighting:** Single soft key from upper left, black negative fill on the
  right to keep the facets readable.
* **Movement:** Imperceptible rotation, 12 s loop.
* **Forbidden:** Wet or glossy surfaces, saturated colour, any suggestion of a
  liquid or a fertiliser blend.

**Rejected because** the granule must physically become the material stream that
carries scenes 03 through 06. A clip cannot hand off to interactive geometry.

## Note on execution

If any of these are ever produced, the workflow requires generating several,
reviewing against the reference photograph, rejecting weak results into
`../rejected/`, and promoting exactly one canonical output into `../selected/`.
Quantity is not the goal, and an approximately-right industrial plate is worse
than no plate at all in a presentation built on verifiability.
