\chapter*{Abstract}
\addcontentsline{toc}{chapter}{Abstract}
This introductory engineering internship addresses an Industrial Engineering problem at OCP Jorf Lasfar: the information used to assess final soluble monoammonium phosphate (MAP) moisture is delivered by laboratory analysis at an interval of approximately two hours, whereas process conditions evolve continuously. The work therefore investigates a digital supervision architecture focused on the drying stage, where final moisture is strongly influenced and product conformity can be affected rapidly.

The contribution is a decision-support \prototype{} that combines a moisture soft sensor, unsupervised anomaly detection, explainable diagnostic logic and a centralized Power BI dashboard. Plant process variables are available continuously through the industrial monitoring environment. For the software demonstration, they are represented on a deliberately selected five-second replay and visualization grid, while laboratory density, product temperature and moisture remain structurally sparse at their approximately two-hour availability. Python performs preprocessing, residence-time-aware alignment, feature computation and inference; PostgreSQL stores idempotent process and model outputs and exposes dashboard-oriented views; Power BI uses DirectQuery to present process KPIs, predicted and measured moisture, anomaly risk, operational severity, probable subsystem, contributors and verification guidance.

The final demonstration uses one deterministic 92-day synthetic source because sufficient real historical and real-time industrial data were not available. The same chronology feeds exploration, feature engineering, moisture-model selection, anomaly modelling and a held-out TEST replay; the runtime loads the exact artifacts exported by Notebooks 03 and 04, including the governed 16-feature moisture contract. The prototype is grounded in the observed soluble MAP process and PCS7 context, but it is not connected to live PCS7, does not command equipment and has not received industrial-scale validation. Its principal result is therefore a tested technical feasibility demonstration and a structured path toward future validation on OCP data.
